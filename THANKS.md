# Thanks

This project is shaped as much by the people who put it through its paces on real
hardware as by the people who write the code. Reverse-engineering a device you can
only observe means someone has to live with the rough edges, capture the logs, and
try the fix again — and again — until it actually holds up on a real home.

## Testers

- **Wingers** — for the endless testing. Thank you for patiently running build after
  build against your own devices, chasing the intermittent cases, sending the logs
  that turned guesses into fixes, and staying with it until the thing was genuinely
  ready. A lot of what works here works because you made sure it did.

If you've helped test and you're not listed here yet, that's an oversight, not intent —
open a PR or say the word and we'll add you.
